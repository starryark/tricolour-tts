# T1: first training round (pre-registered 2026-10-30, before any R/P result or training)

## Arms (each changes one thing from its parent)

| Arm | Init | Speaker rows | Training data |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base | 14 locked rows at 3000–3013 | real train split, effect-screened: 19,829 clips, 20.9 h |
| **C1** | Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice | the same 14 vectors at 3020–3033; presets kept | B1 data + **R** (presets vivian/serena/uncle_fu × no instruct + 5 instructs, generated by untrained CustomVoice, filtered) |
| **C2** | as C1 | as C1 | C1 data + **P** (our 14 characters × 开心/生气/伤心 instructs, generated by untrained CustomVoice with our rows, filtered by Qwen-encoder identity) |

- **Locked vectors:** `tts_work/qwen/speaker_table.pt`, built from 60 screened references per character, drawn in proportion to each game's share of the data. The same vectors go to every arm.
- **Where the rows sit:**
  - B1 checkpoints carry only our 14 speakers.
  - C1/C2 checkpoints keep CustomVoice's 9 presets as well.
  - Row placement is checked by `train/test_equivalence_tri.py`, which passed for both inits across 216 layout combinations and caught a deliberate mutation.

## Training (all arms)
- **Trainer:** `train/train_sft.py` (Kurisu v3 trainer plus instruction prefix, preset rows, speaker-table input, configurable rows).
- **Optimisation:**
  - full fine-tune, fp32 master weights, bf16 autocast;
  - AdamW β (0.9, 0.95), lr 5e-6, weight decay 0.01, grad clip 1.0;
  - warm-up 3%, cosine to 10% over a 2-epoch schedule;
  - effective batch 32 (micro 16 × 2).
- **Model settings:** sub-talker weight 0.3, label alignment fixed, `text_projection` on.
- **Language:** `chinese`, with p_auto 0.25.
- **Run:** max_frames 400, seed 20261030.
- **Checkpoints at 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.25 epochs** (`--save_at`), stopping at 1.25 (`--stop_after`). Validation loss is logged, never used for selection.

## Evaluation suites
Generated on the pod per checkpoint: `language="Chinese"`, default sampling (temperature 0.9, top-k 50, repetition penalty 1.05), non-streaming, max_new_tokens 400. The seed is fixed per (suite, character) batch and identical across checkpoints and arms.

- **S1 held-out:** up to 20 test-split lines per character (≤ 280 clips). No instruction.
- **S2 novel:** the 30 neutral probe sentences × 14 characters (420). No instruction.
- **S3 instruct** (C1/C2 only): S2 texts × 14 characters × {用开心的语气说, 用生气的语气说, 用伤心的语气说} (1,260). The S2 clip of the same character and text is the no-instruction twin.

If pod time runs short, the 0.5-epoch checkpoint is dropped from evaluation first. This is declared now.

## Metrics (scored locally with `eval/judge.py` + ASR)
- **runaway:** ≥ 399 frames, or > 5× the expected duration (CJK characters ÷ 4.5 s).
- **CER:** Paraformer-zh toneless pinyin error vs the text (interjections removed, `b04_verify` scoring), mean over clips. Finalists are confirmed with Qwen3-ASR (a different ASR family).
- **ID:** CAM++ zh-cn 200k, centred nearest-centroid accuracy among the 14 cast centroids, cast mean.
- **SIM:** mean centred cosine to the character's own centroid.
- **ID_instr:** ID on S3, mean over the 3 emotions.
- **LEAK:** share of S1+S2 outputs whose speaker-relative LTAS shows both band edges < −10 dB (the phone detector, against the character's real median).
- **emotion2vec labels:** reported only.
- **Reference points:**
  - real test clips (ID ceiling, real CER on S1 texts, real LEAK rate);
  - untrained Base + vector and untrained CustomVoice + rows (D2) on S2.

## Gates (every candidate checkpoint)
1. **G-run:** runaway ≤ 0.5% on S1+S2, and on S3 for C arms.
2. **G-cer:** CER(S1+S2) ≤ CER of untrained Base + vector on the same suites + 0.02.
3. **G-id:** ID(S1+S2) ≥ 0.90.
4. **G-leak:** LEAK(S1+S2) ≤ the real clean test clips' LEAK rate + 0.01.

## Selection
1. **Within B1:** lowest CER among eligible checkpoints. CERs within 0.005 count as ties; ties go to the higher SIM.
2. **Within C1 ∪ C2:** highest ID_instr among eligible checkpoints. Ties within 0.02 go to the lower CER.
3. **CV winner vs B1 winner:** the CV winner is preferred if it is non-inferior on S1+S2: CER ≤ B1's + 0.01 and ID ≥ B1's − 0.02. Otherwise both are reported to the user as a trade-off (emotion control vs default quality); there is no automatic pick.
4. **No eligible CV checkpoint:** the B1 winner is the model, without emotion control, and this is reported.
5. **Emotion-following claims:** none ships before a blind human A/B on the CV winner: 16 pairs per emotion, S3 vs its S2 twin. An emotion counts as followed if the instructed side wins with an exact 95% binomial lower bound above 50%.
6. **Seed noise:** single seed per arm in T1. Differences between arms smaller than 0.01 CER or 0.02 ID are reported as within noise. The chosen arm is re-trained with a second seed before release if budget allows.

## Budget
- **Estimated pod cost:** one Secure H100, about 3 h, about $12 at about $4/h.
- **Hard stop:** the pod is terminated after the evaluation audio is downloaded. A local watchdog terminates it at 5 h regardless.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-30, after the independent code review, before any R/P result or training)

An independent review (`notes/review_T1.md`) found that the plan could run out of pod time, that the cost safety could fail, and that two selection/scoring biases existed. Changes:

1. **Order and time.**
   - The untrained references B0/C0 are generated first. They are "step-0" checkpoints: the init weights with our rows, written by the trainer's own `save_checkpoint`. Their timing sets the budget.
   - Then, per arm: train, and evaluate checkpoints newest first (1.25 → 1.0 → 0.75 → 0.5).
   - Each finished arm's audio is copied home while the next arm trains.
   - If the projected total exceeds the deadline, the 0.5-epoch checkpoints are skipped first, then the 0.75-epoch ones.
2. **Batching.**
   - Evaluation items are packed across characters (and instructions) into batches of 96, in a fixed order.
   - Each batch is seeded from (group, batch index), identically for every checkpoint and arm. This replaces "seed per (suite, character) batch"; it is still deterministic and paired across checkpoints.
3. **Cost safety.**
   - The watchdog is spawned before the paid call, reconciles a lost create by the pod's unique name, and retries termination until the pod is confirmed gone.
   - The actual pod price is checked against the cap.
   - A pod-side self-destruct runs 30 min after the local deadline, where the image allows it.
   - The deadline is **6 h** (was 5 h), so checkpoints stay on the pod until local selection; the winner is then downloaded.
4. **Judge.**
   - CAM++ centroids come from train-split clips only, never val, test or effect-dropped clips.
   - S1 excludes any line whose text occurs in training (191 lines).
5. **Emotion guard G-emo (C arms).**
   - instr_effect = mean over the 3 emotions of [emotion2vec target rate on S3 − the same emotion's rate on the no-instruction S2 twin].
   - A C checkpoint passes G-emo if instr_effect ≥ 0.5 × C0's instr_effect.
   - G-emo joins the gates for C arms, so selection step 2 cannot pick a checkpoint that ignores instructions in order to maximise ID_instr.
   - emotion2vec's absolute labels stay report-only; this guard uses only its within-model *shift*, on identical texts and characters.
6. **Trainer.** `--no_epoch_checkpoints` skips the redundant epoch-end checkpoint and the non-resumable Adam state (about 20 GB of writes per arm). Nothing else in training changes.
7. **Time rule (fixed before any timing is seen).** C0's evaluation (1,871 clips) measures throughput. If the projected finish of all remaining training and evaluation exceeds the deadline minus 45 min (left for download and selection), C1/C2 are evaluated with `EVAL_CKPTS=2` (the 1.25- and 1.0-epoch checkpoints only). The 1.0 and 1.25 checkpoints of each arm are downloaded as soon as they are written, so a deadline kill never forces a retrain. B1 runs first; C1/C2 start after R/P are uploaded.
